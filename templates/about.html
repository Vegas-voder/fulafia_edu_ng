History

The Faculty of Environmental Design was established in 2011 alongside the inception of the Federal University Lafia. It was created to address the growing need for professionals capable of shaping the built and natural environments in a rapidly developing Nigeria.

Starting with just two departments, the Faculty has expanded over the years, producing graduates who contribute to architecture, urban planning, and environmental sustainability.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a globally recognized Faculty of Environmental Design, renowned for excellence in education, research, and sustainable innovation.

Mission: To empower a diverse community of learners with the knowledge, skills, and values required to design and manage sustainable environments, advancing societal progress and ecological harmony.

Philosophy

Our philosophy is centered on sustainable development and human-centric design. We believe that environmental design is not just about creating structures, but about fostering environments that promote well-being, equity, and ecological balance.
