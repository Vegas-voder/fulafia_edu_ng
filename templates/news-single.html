Faculty Research Colloquium 2025

The annual Faculty Research Colloquium brings together academic staff and postgraduate students to present ongoing research, fostering cross-departmental collaboration and scholarly inquiry.

The Faculty of Environmental Design is pleased to announce the 2025 Faculty Research Colloquium, scheduled for 12th October 2025 at the Faculty Conference Hall, Federal University Lafia. This flagship event brings together researchers, practitioners, and students to explore cutting-edge ideas across architecture, urban planning, estate management, and building technology.

This Year’s Theme

The colloquium is themed “Sustainability and Innovation in the Built Environment.” Participants will present papers and posters across five thematic tracks:

  • Sustainable Architecture and Green Building Design
  • Urban Renewal and Housing Policy in Nigeria
  • Digital Tools in Architectural Practice
  • Real Estate Markets and Infrastructure Development
  • Construction Technology and Materials Innovation
“The Faculty Research Colloquium is more than an academic event — it is a celebration of intellectual curiosity and the power of research to transform the built environment.” — Dean, Faculty of Environmental Design

Keynote Speaker

The colloquium will feature a keynote address by a distinguished architect and urban planner with over two decades of experience in sustainable design practice across West Africa. Details will be announced closer to the date.

How to Participate

Academic staff and postgraduate students are encouraged to submit abstracts for paper presentations. Undergraduates may participate as audience members and poster presenters. Abstract submissions should reach the Faculty Research Committee via Departmental Research Coordinators by 30th September 2025.

For enquiries contact info@fulafia.edu.ng or visit the Faculty Secretariat during working hours.
