Director’s Welcome

A Message from the Director

“Welcome to the Division of Academic Planning and Quality Assurance at Federal University Lafia. Our Division plays a pivotal role in the strategic planning and continuous quality improvement of academic programmes across the university. We are committed to ensuring that FULAFIA meets and surpasses national and international standards in all our academic offerings. Through meticulous planning, data-driven decision making, and stakeholder engagement, we continue to support the university's vision of excellence and innovation.”
Dr. M. A. Suleiman Director, Academic Planning & Quality Assurance
Our Mandate

Core Activities

Strategic Planning

Coordinating university-wide academic plans, monitoring implementation and aligning strategies with national educational goals.

Quality Assurance

Monitoring and evaluating academic programmes to ensure compliance with NUC standards and international best practices.

Data & Statistics

Collecting, analysing, and reporting institutional data to guide evidence-based decision making across the university.

Accreditation

Facilitating NUC accreditation exercises and ensuring all academic programmes maintain full accreditation status.

What We Do

Our Services

Planning

Academic Master Plan Development

We lead the development and periodic review of FULAFIA's Academic Master Plan, ensuring alignment with the university's strategic vision and government policies.

Quality

Programme Review & Benchmarking

Regular programme reviews are conducted to benchmark FULAFIA's offerings against national standards and peer institutions, identifying areas for improvement.

Accreditation

NUC Accreditation Coordination

We coordinate all NUC accreditation exercises, prepare self-study reports, and liaise with faculties to ensure all programmes achieve and maintain accreditation.
