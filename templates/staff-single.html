Biography & Academic Background

Prof. A. Bello is a seasoned Professor of Architecture, distinguished educator, and registered chartered architect. With over 22 years of pedagogical, administrative, and industry practice, he currently serves as the Head of Department of Architecture at Federal University Lafia.

His scholastic inquiry centers on sustainable built environments, climate-responsive indigenous architecture, parametric spatial design, and urban regeneration in West Africa. He has spearheaded multi-disciplinary research initiatives funded by national and international grant bodies, notably leading the TETFund National Research Fund (NRF) initiative on low-carbon construction materials.

As a dedicated mentor, Prof. Bello has successfully supervised numerous doctoral dissertations and master's theses. He serves as an external examiner, accreditation team lead for the National Universities Commission (NUC), and Council Member of the Architects Registration Council of Nigeria (ARCON).
