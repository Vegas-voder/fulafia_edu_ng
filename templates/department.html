About the Department

Learn More About Us

History

The Department of Architecture was established in [year] as part of the Faculty of Environmental Design of the Federal University Lafia. Since its founding, it has grown into one of the most dynamic and forward-thinking architectural programmes in North-Central Nigeria.

Over the years, the department has produced graduates who have gone on to contribute meaningfully to architecture, urban development, and public policy across the country and beyond.

Philosophy

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that architecture is a social art. We train architects who understand that every building, every space, and every city is a reflection of the values, aspirations, and challenges of the people who inhabit them.

We are committed to design excellence, critical thinking, and ethical practice — producing graduates who are agents of positive change in their communities.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a centre of excellence in architectural education, research, and practice in Nigeria and Africa.

Mission: To provide a rigorous, inclusive, and innovative educational environment that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and ethical framework to practice architecture at the highest level.

Curriculum & Requirements

The department offers a five-year professional B.Arch degree programme accredited by the Architects Registration Council of Nigeria (ARCON). The curriculum covers:

  • Architectural Design Studio (Years 1–5)
  • Building Technology and Materials
  • Structures and Environmental Engineering
  • History and Theory of Architecture
  • Professional Practice and Management
  • Urban Design and Housing

Entry requirements include five O’Level credits including Mathematics, Physics, and English Language, plus a satisfactory UTME score and a qualifying post-UTME screening.
