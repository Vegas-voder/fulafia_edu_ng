A Message from the Provost

“Welcome to the College of Natural and Applied Sciences at Federal University Lafia. Our College is dedicated to fostering cutting-edge research, rigorous academic training, and the pursuit of scientific excellence. We equip our students with the analytical tools, laboratory skills, and intellectual curiosity needed to tackle the complex challenges of our time. We warmly invite scholars, researchers, and prospective students to be part of this exciting academic community.”
Prof. A. B. Hassan Provost, College of Natural & Applied Sciences
