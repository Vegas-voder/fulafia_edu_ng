About the Library

Who We Are

History

The University Library was established alongside the Federal University Lafia in 2011. It was designed from inception to be the primary academic and research resource centre for the university community, supporting the teaching, learning, and research activities of all faculties, departments, directorates, and units.

Over the years, the Library has grown significantly in its collections, services, and infrastructure — transitioning from a purely print-based collection to a hybrid library that provides both physical and digital resources to its users.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a world-class academic library that provides innovative, user-centred information services in support of the academic and research excellence of the Federal University Lafia.

Mission: To acquire, organise, preserve, and disseminate relevant information resources in all formats; to provide expert assistance to library users; and to foster a culture of reading, research, and lifelong learning within the FULAFIA community and beyond.

Objectives

The Library pursues the following key objectives:

  • To provide timely and equitable access to information resources in both print and electronic formats.
  • To support the academic programmes of the university through relevant and up-to-date collections.
  • To deliver professional reference, research, and information literacy services.
  • To leverage technology to enhance access to global knowledge resources.
  • To preserve and promote the scholarly output of the Federal University Lafia community.
Services & Resources

The University Library offers a wide range of services to support users at all levels:

  • Circulation Services (borrowing, returns, renewals)
  • Reference and Research Assistance
  • Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC)
  • Access to Electronic Databases and E-Journals
  • Postgraduate and Research Support Services
  • Information Literacy and User Education Programmes
  • Inter-Library Loans and Document Delivery
  • Institutional Repository (FULAFIA ScholarSpace)
  • Reprographic and Printing Services
