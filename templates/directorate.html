About the Directorate

Who We Are

History

The Directorate of Management Information Systems (MIS) was established to coordinate and manage the university’s information and communication technology infrastructure. Since its inception, it has grown into a critical pillar of the university’s operational and academic functions, overseeing the digital transformation of institutional processes.

Over the years, the Directorate has implemented key systems that underpin student records management, financial operations, academic reporting, and administrative communications across all units of the university.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be the foremost technology-driven directorate in Nigerian university administration, enabling a seamless, data-informed, and innovative university experience.

Mission: To provide reliable, secure, and efficient information systems and technology services that support the academic, research, and administrative goals of the Federal University Lafia.

Programmes & Services

The Directorate offers a broad range of technology services and programmes to the university community, including:

  • Student Information System (SIS) Administration
  • Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Support
  • University Website and Portal Management
  • Network Infrastructure and Cybersecurity
  • ICT Training and Capacity Building for Staff
  • Data Analytics and Institutional Reporting
  • Email and Communication Systems Management
