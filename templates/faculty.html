A Message from the Dean

“Welcome to the Faculty of Environmental Design at the Federal University Lafia. Our Faculty is committed to nurturing creative, analytical, and technically sound professionals who are equipped to shape the built environment with integrity and sustainability at the core. Whether you are a prospective student, a researcher, or a partner institution, we invite you to explore the wealth of knowledge, innovation, and community that defines us.”
Prof. Kida Dean, Faculty of Environmental Design
