Dean’s Welcome

A Message from the Dean

“Welcome to the School of Postgraduate Studies at Federal University Lafia. Our School provides a stimulating, intellectually challenging environment where scholars can pursue advanced knowledge, pioneer research, and develop the expertise required to address the most pressing challenges of our time. We offer a diverse range of postgraduate programmes designed to sharpen your analytical and research skills. I invite you to explore the opportunities we offer and join our vibrant community of scholars.”
Prof. S. A. Idris Dean, School of Postgraduate Studies
Our Mandate

What We Stand For

Academic Excellence

Maintaining the highest standards in postgraduate teaching, research, and scholarship across all disciplines.

Research Leadership

Driving innovative research that contributes to national development, regional growth, and global knowledge.

Capacity Building

Developing skilled, ethical, and globally competitive postgraduate professionals who lead in their fields.

Community Engagement

Translating research outputs into practical solutions that address societal challenges across Nasarawa State and Nigeria.
