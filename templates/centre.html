About the Centre

Who We Are

History

The Centre for Entrepreneurial Development (CED) was established by the Federal University Lafia to serve as the primary hub for fostering an entrepreneurial culture across all faculties and departments of the university. It was founded in response to the growing need to equip graduates with not just academic qualifications, but the enterprise skills required to create jobs and drive economic development.

Since its establishment, the Centre has trained hundreds of students, supported emerging startups, and partnered with government agencies, NGOs, and private sector organisations to create opportunities for entrepreneurship and self-reliance among graduates.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a leading centre of excellence in entrepreneurship education and enterprise development in North-Central Nigeria and beyond.

Mission: To cultivate an entrepreneurial mindset among students and staff of the Federal University Lafia through structured training, mentoring, incubation support, and strategic industry partnerships — producing graduates who are job creators, not job seekers.

Programmes & Services

The Centre offers a range of programmes and services to support aspiring and early-stage entrepreneurs, including:

  • Entrepreneurship Education and Training (EET) for all undergraduates
  • Business Incubation and Startup Support
  • Mentorship and Coaching from Industry Professionals
  • Access to Seed Funding and Grant Information
  • Innovation Competitions and Business Plan Challenges
  • Industry Linkages and Partnership Programmes
  • Entrepreneurship Seminars, Workshops, and Bootcamps
